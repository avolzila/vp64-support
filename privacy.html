Privacy policy

This policy covers the Poker 64 app and this support website.

Information handled by the app

Poker 64 runs locally on your device. The app does not require an account, connect to a game server, show ads, use analytics, or send gameplay information to us. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

Cards and virtual credits are held for the current play session. They are not transmitted to us and disappear when the app closes. We cannot recover them. The credits have no cash value and cannot be redeemed.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request. Support messages sent to contact@avolzila.com are forwarded to our mailbox. We do not sell support information or use it for advertising.

We keep support messages while needed to resolve the request and any follow-up, then delete them during normal mailbox cleanup. You can request deletion of your support correspondence at any time by emailing contact@avolzila.com. We may need to retain information when required by law.

App Store and website hosting

Apple may process app downloads, purchases, and diagnostics under its own privacy policy. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process site visits under its privacy statement. The site itself does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or contact forms.

Changes and questions

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email contact@avolzila.com.
