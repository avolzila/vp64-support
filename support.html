Support

For help, feedback, or a bug report, email contact@avolzila.com.

Include your device model, operating system version, app version, and what happened. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Playing

Tap CREDIT to add free virtual credits. Use BET to change the wager, then DEAL to draw five cards. Tap HOLD below any cards you want to keep, then DEAL again. After a win, choose COLLECT or DOUBLE. Available choices blink on the cabinet.

Troubleshooting

  • No sound: Check device volume and mute settings, then relaunch the app.
  • Cabinet does not fit: Rotate iPhone or iPad to landscape. On Mac, resize the window or use full screen.
  • Credits disappeared: Credits and hands belong to the current play session. Closing the app starts a fresh game.
